How it works

Private by design,
not by policy.

PhantomPen doesn't ask you to trust it. The encryption happens on your device before anything is stored — there's nothing to leak.

01
Set your passphrase

Derives an AES-256 key using PBKDF2 with 200,000 iterations. Never stored, never sent anywhere.

02
Write anything

Rich text editor with autosave. Every keystroke is encrypted before it hits localStorage.

03
AI assists locally

Ollama runs a language model on your machine. Your prompts never leave your device.

04
Share securely

Encrypted share links with the key in the URL fragment — the server never sees it.
